## 释义

**settle** /ˈsetl/ *v.*  这里是 **settle (for)**：将就、凑合着接受一个不够好的东西。

settle 本义是"安定下来"（*settle down*、*settle in a city*）。"安定"用在选择上，就成了"不再找了，就这个吧"——也就是将就。

乔布斯讲的是找工作和找伴侣：*the only way to do great work is to love what you do... keep looking. Don't settle.*

## 原句

> So keep looking until you find it. Don't settle.
>
> — Steve Jobs, Stanford Commencement Address, 2005 · [视频 08:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UF8uR6Z6KLc&t=505s)

## 用法笔记

- *settle for less / for second best* 将就、退而求其次
- *Don't settle.* 已经成了一句独立的励志口号
- 其他常见义：*settle a dispute / a bill*（解决争端 / 结清账单）、*the dust settled*（尘埃落定）
- 名词 **settlement**：定居点；和解协议
